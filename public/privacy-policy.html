Privacy Policy

Application: TheSurvey | Effective Date: October 7, 2026

Official Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how TheSurvey ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by tri3a, collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our mobile application.

By using TheSurvey, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal data is handled securely and responsibly.

1. Information We Collect

To provide core surveyor and field data-collection functionalities, TheSurvey requests access to specific permissions and data on your device:

  • Precise & Approximate Location (GPS): We collect foreground location data (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) to accurately tag and verify the geographic coordinates of surveyed points, properties, or objects in real time.
  • Camera: We request access to your device's camera (CAMERA) to allow surveyors to capture photos directly within the app as visual proof and documentation for survey submissions.
  • Photos & Media Storage: We request storage/media access (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) to allow you to attach existing images from your gallery to survey forms and to save captured media files locally.
  • Audio / Microphone: We access the device microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) solely when you record voice notes or audio attachments related to survey assessments.
  • Device & Application Data: We collect app version, device model, and operating system version for diagnostic and operational support.

2. How We Use Your Information

All information gathered by TheSurvey is used strictly for App Functionality and surveyor operations:

  • Verifying that the surveyor is physically present at the designated survey location.
  • Associating photographic and audio evidence with the correct survey questionnaire responses.
  • Enabling offline mode functionality, allowing survey records to be cached locally on the device until an internet connection is established.
  • Synchronizing completed survey submissions with our secure organizational server/backend API.
Data Retention & Storage:

Survey responses, coordinates, photos, and audio files are stored in local device storage for offline capability and uploaded to our backend servers upon synchronization. We do not sell or monetize your data.

3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Disclosures

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data, media, or location coordinates with third-party advertising networks or external data brokers.

Data collected is transmitted solely to the designated backend infrastructure authorized by your organization for processing and report generation.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security safeguards to protect your data. All data transmissions between the application and our servers are encrypted using Secure Socket Layer / Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/SSL). Local data stored on the device is protected by operating system sandboxing.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Requests

We retain survey information only for as long as necessary to fulfill organizational reporting and administrative purposes.

If you wish to request the deletion of your account, survey records, or any personal data associated with your user identity, you may submit a request by contacting us at the email address provided below. We will process and confirm your deletion request in accordance with applicable laws.

6. Children's Privacy

TheSurvey is a professional enterprise and surveyor tool intended for use by authorized personnel aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify users of any material changes by posting the updated Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top of the policy.
